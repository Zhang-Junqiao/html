Practical Tips

How to get there, where to stay, how much to budget

Transport

ModeRouteTime / Cost
High-speed railHangzhou East → Wenzhou SouthAbout 110 RMB per person
TrainWenzhou South → Yandangshan StationAbout 30 min, 25 RMB
Self-drive / chartered carDowntown → Nanxi RiverAbout 1 hour

Accommodation

Stay near Wuma Street. Budget hotels cost 150–280 RMB per night, within walking distance to Jiangxin Island ferry and Wuma Street.

Budget

  • 4 days 3 nights: about 1600 RMB per person
  • 3 days 2 nights: 1100–1300 RMB per person

2026 Free-Ticket Policy

Show your flight or high-speed rail ticket arriving in Wenzhou within 7 days to get free first-entry tickets to 26 state-owned scenic spots, including Jiangxin Island and Yandang Mountain.
